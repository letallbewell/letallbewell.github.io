Make and Fly Cheap RC Airplanes

I am a massive fan of all flying things, especially birds. I dream to imitate flight on a budget. I built a remote-controlled aircraft adhering to the bare minimum for sustained flight and control. I dare to call this one of the cheapest remote-controlled flying structures; the cost is not far from the price of a brushless motor and a Lithium-polymer battery. The control signals are transmitted using RF modules, not a reliable way to control the plane, but as you might have guessed, it is ridiculously cheap.
